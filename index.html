How it works

Golf, minus the boring bits.

Traditional golf takes four-plus hours and a bag full of clubs. Golfsies takes an hour after work, three clubs and your best mates.

Build your crew

Teams of 4 on the course, with up to 6 on your roster so nobody's stuck when someone can't make it.

Grab 3 clubs

Pick any three: a wood, a 7-iron and a putter works well. No clubs? We've got loan sets.

Play 6 short holes

Team scramble on holes of 100–130m. Everyone hits, you play the best shot, and the round's done in under an hour.

Hit the Party Hole

Hole 6 is right by the clubhouse, with music and double points. Then stay for a drink and check the ladder.

The rules

Simple enough for first-timers.
Competitive enough for everyone.

Easy-play rules keep things moving and keep it fun, whatever your handicap (or lack of one).

3

Club limit

Everyone carries a maximum of three clubs. It levels the field and keeps play quick.

1m

No-penalty drop

Missed the fairway? Drop it one metre back onto the short grass with no penalty, and keep moving.

2×

Party Hole points

Hole 6 is worth double, so no match is over until the last putt drops.

The season

10 weeks. One trophy. Bragging rights for a year.

Head-to-head team matches every week on a twilight weeknight, with ladder points, finals and a grand final.

FAQ

Questions? Fair enough.

Do I need to be good at golf?

Not at all. The team scramble means everyone plays the best shot, so beginners and experienced golfers contribute on every hole. Plenty of players have never played a full round before.

Do I need my own clubs?

You need three at most. If you don't have any, we'll have loan sets at the course.

Where and when is it played?

On twilight weeknights at a Gold Coast course, finishing before dark. We're locking in venues now. Register your interest and you'll be the first to hear.

How much does it cost?

Pricing will be released when registrations open. Teams who register interest now will get early-bird pricing.

What if I don't have a team?

Register as a solo player or a pair and we'll match you with others to make up a team.

Are uniforms compulsory?

Yes, it's half the fun. Each team gets a matching kit, made to order before the season starts.

I run a golf course or club. Can we host Golfsies?

We'd love to talk. Golfsies brings new players onto your course on quiet weeknights, and you keep the bar takings. Email rohan@golfsies.com.au.
