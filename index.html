FAQ

What do I get with a team membership?

Your team name and crest reserved in Golfsies, a team page, a roster of up to 8 players, members-only tournament entry, ranking points at every event, and member kit pricing.

Do I pay anything now?

No. Reserving a Founding Team name is free. We'll confirm pricing and send the membership terms before the first event, and you only pay once you choose to join.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Memberships are month to month, and the full terms, including how to cancel, will be sent before any payment is taken.

Who owns the team?

The person who takes out the membership is the team owner. They hold the right to that team name and crest within Golfsies, and they manage the roster. Golfsies® itself remains a registered trade mark of Golfsies.

Do I need to be good at golf?

No. In a team scramble everyone plays the best shot, so beginners and regular golfers contribute on every hole.

Do I need my own clubs?

You only need three. If you don't have any, we'll have loan sets at the course.

Where and when are tournaments played?

At Gold Coast courses, on twilight weeknights in summer and weekend afternoons in winter. We're locking in venues now, and Founding Teams will hear first.

How does scoring work?

Each team plays a scramble and scores points on every hole: 1 for a bogey, 2 for a par and 3 for a birdie. The Party Hole doubles your points. The team with the most points wins the tournament.

How do ranking points work?

Every team earns ranking points at each tournament based on where it finishes, plus points for turning up. The top-ranked teams at the end of the season qualify for the Golfsies Championship.

What are the prizes?

Trophies, kit, sponsor prizes and the bragging rights that come with them. Details will be announced for each event.

What if I don't have a team?

Register as a solo player or a pair and we'll match you with others to make up a team.
