THE IMPORTANT BITS

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Does Golfsies book my golf course?

No. For private games, you organise your own tee time and pay any course fees directly to the venue. Check the course permits the format you want to play.

Do I need to play six holes?

No. Six Hole Showdown is the signature format, but other challenges can be played at suitable approved practice facilities or courses.

Is this a live scoring platform?

Not yet. This site is a concept preview. The game creator generates a shareable invitation, and the example scorecard works locally on your device. Shared accounts and synchronised leaderboards are not live.

Are corporate events available to purchase?

Not yet. The $499 package is indicative. You can enquire by email to help shape the offering before launch.

Will this work at any golf club?

Only where the venue permits your chosen activity. Always follow course rules, approved playing areas, safety directions and standard booking arrangements.
