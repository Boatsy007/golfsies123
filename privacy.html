Privacy notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Golfsies is a proposed social golf league based on the Gold Coast, Queensland. This notice explains how we intend to use information you share when contacting us or requesting a Founding Team name.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, email address, mobile number, preferred team names, suburb, team size, experience, preferred playing days and any optional comments you provide.

Why we collect it

We use the details to respond to enquiries, review team-name requests, contact you about proposed launch events and, where appropriate, provide information about membership. Requesting a name does not create a paid membership or guarantee that a name is available.

How requests are received

Until an online submissions service is connected, the website prepares an email in your own email application. You must press Send yourself. Golfsies receives only emails successfully sent to us. We may later use a third-party form or hosting service and will update this notice when that changes.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell enquiry details. Information may be processed by our email and website service providers to deliver our communications and run the website. We keep information only as long as reasonably needed to manage enquiries or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal details we hold about your enquiry, or ask us to stop sending updates. Contact rohan@golfsies.com.au.

Questions

For privacy enquiries, please email rohan@golfsies.com.au.
